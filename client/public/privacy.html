SpecHarvest privacy policy

Applies to the SpecHarvest Android app and website. Last updated: 12 October 2026.

SpecHarvest reads product listings from online shops you choose, turns them into a searchable collection of products and their specifications, and lets you search and compare them. This page explains what data that takes and what happens to it.

Who runs it

The app connects to a SpecHarvest server. By default that's specharvest.bootta.dev, run by the app's developer. You can also enter another server on the sign-in screen. Then that server's operator holds your data, under their own terms, and this policy only covers the app itself.

Questions or requests: CONTACT_EMAIL.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and your password, stored only as a salted hash.
  • Your content: the shop pages you ask it to read, and the collections, products, specifications, groups and searches made from them.
  • Keys and settings: AI provider API keys you add (encrypted), API keys you create, and your notification settings (for example an ntfy topic or a Telegram chat).
  • Usage: which jobs you ran and what the AI calls cost, so you can see your spending and the server can enforce limits.
  • Security logs: request logs with IP addresses, kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service.

On your phone, the app keeps your sign-in tokens and the server address in its own storage. It asks for permission to show notifications, so it can tell you when a crawl or lookup finishes.

How it's used and shared

  • Your data is used only to provide SpecHarvest to you: crawl, extract, search and notify.
  • To read product details, page text is sent to the AI provider in use (for example OpenRouter, OpenAI or Google), with your own key or the server's. Web lookups you start send search queries to a search provider. They process it to answer that request.
  • Notifications go to the channels you set up yourself.
  • Collections are private unless you mark one as shared. Then other users of the same server can search it.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics SDKs, and your data is never sold.
  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Delete your account

In the app or on the website:

  1. Sign in, then tap the round account button (your initial, top right) → Account & API keys.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete my account…
  3. Enter your current password and tap Delete forever.

This deletes, right away and for good: your account, your collections and their products (shared ones too), groups, search history, API keys, AI provider keys, connected browsers, notification settings and sign-ins. Export collections you want to keep first.

Cost records of AI calls are kept without your email or account, for the server's own accounting. Copies in server backups go away as old backups are replaced.

Can't sign in any more, or uninstalled the app? Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address of your account and we'll delete it for you.

Children

SpecHarvest is not meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced in the app.
